# --- Tutorial Island ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Gielinor Guide's house, where a new account starts: x1,y1,x2,y2 in world tiles. It's

# A square round Lumbridge's spawn, kept clear (and fenced) while a new account is set up

3218,3214,3226,3222

[welcome]
Welcome to ChunkBlazer
You've just unlocked your first chunk. ChunkBlazer splits the world into square chunks, 64 tiles across, and you can only use the ones you've unlocked.

Everything else is locked. Click the world map button by your minimap to see.

[map]
The world map
Your chunk is in full colour; locked chunks are shaded dark.

Close the map to carry on.

[walls|Next]
Your first chunk
The glowing walls mark the edge of what you've unlocked: stay inside them.

While you're on Tutorial Island, every chunk you walk into unlocks for free, so you can see how it works. On the mainland you'll unlock them with points, and only the ones next to a chunk you already own.

[tasks|Deal my tasks]
Tasks and points
Every chunk you unlock comes with tasks: catch a fish, beat a monster, make an item. Finish tasks to earn points, then spend points to unlock more chunks.

Here are some practice tasks for the island. They're worth 0 points, they just show you how it works.

[deal]
Your practice tasks
New tasks are dealt as face-down cards. Click each card to flip it, then click it again to send it to your task list.

[iorb]
Your task list
Click the fire orb by your minimap to open your task list. Tasks you haven't looked at yet are under New tasks.

[itrack]
Track a task
Click the task to track it.

[iclose]
Close the task viewer
Hit Esc or click the cross to close the task viewer.

[practice]
Practice tasks
Do your practice tasks as you go through the island, the way you would on the mainland. Each one is worth 0 points.

[newchunk|Got it]
A new chunk
You've walked into another locked chunk. On the mainland you'd need to unlock it first, or you can't do anything in it.

Here on Tutorial Island it unlocks for free.

[tokens|Got it]
Boss Tokens
Bosses live in boss chunks, which you unlock with Boss Tokens instead of points. You start with 2.

Unlocking a boss chunk gives you all of its tasks at once, and beating its boss for the first time earns you a token.

[mode]
Choose how you'll play
This choice is permanent for this account.

[casual]
Casual
Play on any account, starting anywhere. Shown on the casual leaderboard.

[competitive]
Competitive
For a new ironman account, Group included. Shown on the main leaderboard and the website. Needs Server Sync to check your account.

[leave]
On to the mainland
That's the practice done. Finish Tutorial Island as normal: you'll choose how to play ChunkBlazer once you reach the mainland.

[arrive|Choose]
Welcome to the mainland
Your practice chunks and tasks are gone: from here on it counts.

Now you can type in chat, it's time to set up your account: Server Sync first, then how you'll play. Then you'll unlock Lumbridge, your first chunk.

[sync]
Server Sync
Server Sync saves your progress to chunkblazer.com under your RuneScape name: for the leaderboards, your profile, and playing on more than one computer. Competitive needs it, to check your account. You can turn it on later from the side panel.

[node|Got it]
Group ironman
Every member of your group plays their own ChunkBlazer: your own chunks, tasks and points. Your progress isn't shared with the rest of your group.

# --- Starting from Lumbridge (the plugin turned on outside Tutorial Island) --------------

[lwelcome|Next]
Welcome to ChunkBlazer
ChunkBlazer splits the world into square chunks, 64 tiles across. You can only use the chunks you've unlocked.

You start with Lumbridge for free, plus the chunk you're standing in if you play Casual: you'll unlock them once your account is set up. Until then everything is locked: shaded dark on your minimap and world map, with glowing walls at the edges.

[lunlock|Next]
Unlocking chunks
You unlock chunks with points, and only the ones next to a chunk you already own. Each chunk costs a little more than the last.

[ltasks|Next]
Tasks and points
Every chunk you unlock comes with tasks: catch a fish, beat a monster, make an item. Finish tasks to earn points, then spend points to unlock more chunks.

[ltokens|Next]
Boss Tokens
Bosses live in boss chunks, which you unlock with Boss Tokens instead of points. You start with 2.

Unlocking a boss chunk gives you all of its tasks at once, and beating its boss for the first time earns you a token.

[group|Next]
Group ironman
Every member of your group plays their own ChunkBlazer: your own chunks, tasks and points. Your progress isn't shared with the rest of your group.

[home|Unlock Lumbridge]
Your first chunk
You're all set. Lumbridge is your free starting chunk (plus the chunk you're standing in, on Casual): unlock it to start, and your first tasks are dealt.

[verify|Choose again]
Setting up your account
Picked Casual? This only takes a moment.

Picked Competitive? The server checks your account first. When a code appears in your chat box, type it in public chat to confirm this account is yours.

# --- The game's interface, once a mode is picked ------------------------------------------

[cards]
Your first tasks
Your first tasks have been dealt as cards. Click each card to flip it, then click it again to put it away.

[orb]
Your task list
Click the fire orb by your minimap to open your task list.

[track]
Track a task
Click the task to track it.

[close]
Close the task viewer
Hit Esc or click the cross to close the task viewer.

[tracker|Next]
The task box
Your tracked task shows in a box on your screen, with its progress. Hover it to see what to do.

[unlocking|Next]
Unlocking more chunks
When you have enough points, walk through the glowing wall into a chunk next to yours: you'll be asked whether to unlock it, and it opens up around you.

[save]
Save a task
Right-click the task you're tracking (or click its star) to save it, so it's easy to find again. (Open your task list with the fire orb first.)

[sclose]
Close the task viewer
Hit Esc or click the cross to close the task viewer.

[boss]
Your saved tasks
Click the saved tasks button (the one with the paper on it) to see the tasks you've saved. The dragon orb by your minimap counts your Boss Tokens.

[panel|Next]
The side panel
The ChunkBlazer side panel, the fire icon on RuneLite's sidebar, lists your tasks too, with search and filters, plus Server Sync and your account settings.

[done|Finish]
You're ready
Finish tasks, earn points, unlock chunks, and see how far you get.
